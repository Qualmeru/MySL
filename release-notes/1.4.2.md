# MySL 1.4.2 – Filters for quieter times

- Lines and directions previously seen at a stop remain available in its filters, even when no departure is imminent.
- Hide a line by entering its number, and restore hidden lines and directions individually.

## Install

Download **MySL.apk** (or **MySL-preset.apk** for the version with preset stops) below. Updating keeps your favourites and filters.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/Qualmeru/MySL/compare/1.4.1...1.4.2
