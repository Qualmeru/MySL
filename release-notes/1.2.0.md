# MySL 1.2.0 – Keep your favourites, no login needed

## What's new

### Your favourites are backed up
- Favourites, their filters and your settings are now part of Android's own backup to the phone's Google account. They come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. There's no login in the app.
- Only what matters is backed up: departures, widget setup and other temporary data are left out.

### Share favourites with another phone
- **⋮ → Share favourites** sends your stops and their filters as one line of text, by message, e-mail or anything else.
- **⋮ → Import favourites** on the other phone: paste the whole message. The stops are added to the ones you have, and a stop you already have takes the imported filters.

### Filters
- Each stop's header now also says how many departures your filters are hiding right now, e.g. "1 direction hidden · 2 lines hidden · 5 departures hidden".

### Smaller
- The APK is about 1.9 MB.

## Install

Download **MySL.apk** (or **MySL-preset.apk** for the version with Frihetsvägen and Helenelund preset) below, open it on your Android phone and allow installing from that source when asked. Updating keeps your favourites and filters.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/Qualmeru/MySL/compare/1.1.0...1.2.0
