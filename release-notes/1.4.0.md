# MySL 1.4.0 – Alerts when you want them, a widget for any stop

## What's new

### Alerts that fit your day
- **⋮ → Alert times and notices** sets when disruption alerts may come: only between certain hours (e.g. 06:00–09:00, or overnight like 22:00–06:00) and/or only on weekdays. Anything still going on when that time starts is announced then.
- **Only what matters.** SL's general information, like "occasional delays because of slippery roads", no longer sends an alert. Turn on **Minor notices too** in the same place if you want those.
- **Per stop.** Each stop's ⋮ menu has **Alerts for this stop**. Turn it off for a stop you only look at now and then; its header then says "alerts off".
- **No double alerts for a cancellation.** You get one clear "178 to Mörby at 15:34 is cancelled", not that and a bare "Inställd" as well.

### One-stop widget for any stop
- When placing the *One stop* widget, **Search all stops…** lets you pick any of SL's stops, not only your favourites.
- The widget keeps showing its stop even after you remove that stop from your favourites. A favourite's direction and line filters still apply to it.
- A widget without a stop opens the picker when you tap it. On Android 12 and later you can also long-press the widget to choose another stop.

### Under the hood
- Built with the latest Android tools and libraries, and made for Android 16.

## Install

Download **MySL.apk** (or **MySL-preset.apk** for the version with Frihetsvägen and Helenelund preset) below, open it on your Android phone and allow installing from that source when asked. Updating keeps your favourites and filters. If you have 1.3.0, the app offers this update itself within a day.
