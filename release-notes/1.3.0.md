# MySL 1.3.0 – Own icon, update notices and a steadier app

## What's new

### Own app icon
- MySL now has its own icon: a white bus on SL blue, easy to spot in the launcher and the widget list. On Android 13+ it also follows your phone's themed icons.

### Know when there's a new version
- Once a day the app checks for a newer release here. If there is one, a banner at the top offers **Download** or **Not now**, so you no longer need to look for updates yourself.

### Steadier when SL is busy
- When SL answers "Quota has been exceeded", the app, the widgets and the disruption alerts now all pause together for a while (a minute at first, up to 15) instead of each retrying on its own and keeping SL busy. The first good answer ends the pause.

### Smaller fixes
- **Turning the phone** no longer empties the list and reloads every stop.
- **The widgets follow the app's theme.** If MySL is set to always light or always dark, the widgets are too, and they change right away when you change theme or language in the app.
- **TalkBack** reads each departure as one sentence, e.g. "Bus 178 towards Jakobsberg, 7 min, 15:34, Stand A", in the app and in the widgets.

## Install

Download **MySL.apk** (or **MySL-preset.apk** for the version with Frihetsvägen and Helenelund preset) below, open it on your Android phone and allow installing from that source when asked. Updating keeps your favourites and filters.

From this version on, the app tells you about the next update itself.
