# MySL 1.4.1 – Departure countdowns and widget refresh

- Departure times now show the remaining minutes above the clock time in the app and widgets.
- Widget refresh has a larger tap target, shows when it is refreshing, and reports connection errors.

## Install

Download **MySL.apk** (or **MySL-preset.apk** for the version with preset stops) below. Updating keeps your favourites and filters.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/Qualmeru/MySL/compare/1.4.0...1.4.1
